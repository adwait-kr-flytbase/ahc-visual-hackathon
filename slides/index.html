Lower false-alarm, for free

One entrant on this leaderboard lost 26 of 35 marks to 94 false alarms.

AND THE OBVIOUS FIX THAT DOES NOT WORK

Gating on the model's own confidence, 0.5 → 0.9, removes 2 false alarms out of 31. Confidence carries no signal — whatever gates false alarms must come from outside the model.

Seven silent failures in seven hours

None crashed. Every one would have read as “the model just isn't very good.”

So we validate intermediate artifacts, not outputs. #7 was caught only by the diagnostic added for #4. Each is now a test.

WE RETRACTED OUR OWN HEADLINE, BEFORE IT SHIPPED

“Bigger is worse — the 4B beats the 8B, F1 0.61 vs 0.48.” The two models differ on 3 videos out of 24; sign test p = 0.25. The gap was 10 TP vs 8 TP — inside the ±1 TP noise band we had been telling everyone to apply, and then failed to apply ourselves.

A NEGATIVE RESULT, TESTED PROPERLY
0.556 AUC · CHANCE IS 0.500

Codec motion vectors as an ego-motion-robust gate — free from the decoder, the idea every fixed-camera paper implies. Against a 2,000-trial permutation null it does not work. Settled in 1,165 CPU-seconds, no GPU.
